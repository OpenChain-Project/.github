# Election Process and Policy for OpenChain

OpenChain seeks to hold open, fair, transparent, and efficient elections for various roles within the OpenChain. See the chart before for the elections and role timelines for OpenChain.

| Role | Role Period | Electorate Group | Number of Elected Individuals | Other Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Governing Board Chairperson | 3 year terms beginning Jan 1 and ending Dec 31 of the third year | Governing Board Representatives | One ( see [OpenChain Project Charter, section 3(b)(ii)](https://charter.openchainproject.org) ) | 3 year term |
| Work Group Chairperson | 1 year | Governing Board Representatives | One Chairperson or Two Co-Chairpersons | Governing Board takes the recommendations of the Work Group and formally ratifies. |

Elections are typically held in the month before the end date of each of the roles above. The exact timeline will be communicated to the electorate group, but it generally has the following structure:

| Election Notice | 2 weeks |
| :---- | :---- |
| Nomination Period | 1 week, closes at 11:59 pm \<TIMEZONE\> on the last day |
| Review of Nominees | 1 day |
| Election Period | 1 week, closes at 11:59 pm \<TIMEZONE\> on the last day |
| Announcement of Election results | Day after Election Ends |

## Eligibility

To serve on any governing body, council, or committee of the OpenChain, an individual must:

* Be willing and able to fulfill the applicable responsibilities, and  
* Comply with all applicable policies of the Linux Foundation and the OpenChain, including Linux Foundation Antitrust Policy and OpenChain Code of Conduct.

Failure to meet these requirements may result in removal, suspension, or ineligibility to participate.

## Nominations

Nominations are done via email. Members of the electorate group may nominate someone else in the electorate group or self-nominate. All nominations must include the nominee’s name, company affiliation, and a short background on the nominee and why they wish to serve in the role. All nominations are confirmed before the election.

## Elections

The election process uses [LFX Voting](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/project-control-center/v2-latest-version/collaborations/voting#voting-for-a-poll). Electors will checkbox the individual(s) they wish to elect.

Election results will be announced to the electorate and relevant committee the day after the election closes.

If you have any questions about the election process, please contact the OpenChain staff at <support@openchainproject.org>.
